Everyday emotions, independently learned

A multilabel model trained on Google Research’s GoEmotions dataset can suggest more than one emotion. Happy / joyful maps to joy; excited / enthusiastic maps to excitement. Labels do not compete for a single 100% total.

Tired / low energy is a separate phrase cue for explicit tiredness wording. It is not a learned fatigue classifier and can miss paraphrases or context.
